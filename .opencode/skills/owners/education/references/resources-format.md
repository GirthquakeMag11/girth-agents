# RESOURCES.md format

`RESOURCES.md` is the curated set of trusted sources for the topic. Lesson
knowledge is drawn from here, never from the model's memory. Wisdom comes
from the communities listed here.

```markdown
# {Topic} Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Title_ by Author](https://…)
  What it covers precisely, and when to reach for it.
- [Article: "Title" by Author (Source)](https://…)
  What it covers precisely, and when to reach for it.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Community](https://…)
  Why it is high-signal, and what to take there.
- Local: {the place}
  What to take there.
```

The rules:

- **High-trust only.** Primary sources, recognised experts, peer-reviewed
  work, strongly moderated communities. Marketing dressed as education stays
  out.
- **Annotate every entry.** A bare link is useless in three months: one line
  saying what it covers and when to reach for it.
- **Group by Knowledge / Wisdom.** A resource may appear in only one group.
- **Surface gaps explicitly.** Where no good resource exists for something
  the mission needs, a `## Gaps` section says so — it drives future search.
- **Prune ruthlessly.** Wrong, shallow, or off-mission sources get removed,
  not buried.
- **Record community preferences.** A learner who opted out of communities
  gets a note in `NOTES.md` so future sessions stop proposing them.
