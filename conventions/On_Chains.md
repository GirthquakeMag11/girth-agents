# On_Chains

- **2026-10-20 — Chains describe, they never bind** — The skill-chain map
  in README.md shows the shape work usually takes: which door a person
  runs, which skills fire when their trigger matches, where records land
  and feed later steps. An edge means "typically leads to", never "must";
  nothing on a chart may forbid a move. A skill fires whenever its trigger
  matches, wherever the agent stands.
- **2026-10-20 — Recovery is always a step away** — Every chart must
  leave the four recovery rails reachable from every node, and no rule may
  close one: record the stray (tracking), settle a meaning or ruling
  (codifying), surface open items (/inbox), re-check the scaffold
  (/bootstrap).
- **2026-10-20 — Commands are the doors, skills the machinery** — A
  command exists only where the act is the person's to make: approve a
  scaffold, settle a spec, call a release, open or close the inbox.
  Everywhere else the skill fires on its trigger; the person never summons
  one.
